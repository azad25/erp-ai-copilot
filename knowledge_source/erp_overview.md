# ERP System Overview

## What is the ERP System?

The ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) System is a comprehensive business management platform that integrates all aspects of your organization's operations into a single, unified system.

## Core Modules

### 1. Sales Module
The Sales Module manages the entire sales lifecycle:
- **Order Management**: Create, track, and fulfill customer orders
- **Quote Generation**: Generate professional quotes and proposals
- **Invoice Processing**: Automated invoice generation and tracking
- **Customer Relationship Tracking**: Maintain detailed customer interaction history
- **Sales Analytics**: Real-time sales performance dashboards

### 2. Inventory Module
Comprehensive inventory management capabilities:
- **Stock Management**: Real-time inventory tracking across multiple warehouses
- **Product Catalog**: Centralized product information management
- **Stock Alerts**: Automated low-stock notifications
- **Inventory Valuation**: FIFO, LIFO, and weighted average costing methods
- **Barcode Integration**: Scan and track products efficiently

### 3. Finance & Accounting Module
Complete financial management:
- **General Ledger**: Double-entry accounting system
- **Accounts Payable**: Vendor bill management and payment processing
- **Accounts Receivable**: Customer invoice tracking and collections
- **Bank Reconciliation**: Automated bank statement matching
- **Financial Reporting**: Balance sheets, P&L statements, cash flow reports

### 4. Human Resources Module
Employee lifecycle management:
- **Employee Records**: Comprehensive employee information database
- **Payroll Processing**: Automated salary calculations and disbursements
- **Leave Management**: Track vacation, sick leave, and time off
- **Performance Reviews**: Structured performance evaluation system
- **Recruitment**: Applicant tracking and hiring workflows

### 5. Project Management Module
Project planning and execution:
- **Project Planning**: Define project scope, timeline, and resources
- **Task Management**: Assign and track project tasks
- **Time Tracking**: Log hours spent on projects and tasks
- **Resource Allocation**: Optimize team member assignments
- **Project Reporting**: Progress tracking and milestone reporting

### 6. CRM (Customer Relationship Management)
Customer engagement and relationship building:
- **Contact Management**: Centralized customer and lead database
- **Lead Tracking**: Sales pipeline and opportunity management
- **Communication History**: Email, call, and meeting logs
- **Marketing Campaigns**: Campaign planning and execution
- **Customer Support**: Ticket management and issue resolution

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I create a new sales order?**
A: Navigate to Sales > Orders > New Order, fill in customer details and line items, then save.

**Q: Can I customize the dashboard?**
A: Yes, click the "Customize" button on the dashboard to add, remove, or rearrange widgets.

**Q: How do I generate a financial report?**
A: Go to Finance > Reports, select the report type, choose date range, and click "Generate".

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption, regular backups, and strict access controls.

**Q: Can I access the system on mobile?**
A: Yes, the system is fully responsive and works on all devices.
